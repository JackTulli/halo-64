Halo 64 texture picker: map by map

What the options are

Pick for each map on its own: choose the map at the top. A texture on several maps shows where else it is; Use on every map copies this map's pick (and its dithering) to all of them. Blue map names: that map's pick is different.

Level and Objects: full colour (RGBA16), 16 colours (CI4), greyscale (I4, 16 shades; IA4 with 8 shades for cut-outs) and greyscale tinted (one colour on the vertices). A texture with the player's colour keeps it only in 16 colours. The level's transparent textures stay in colour. First-person weapons and hands are not here.

Ground grain: the detail drawn over the ground's colour tiles (2 KB at most beside the tile), shown over the ground's average colour. Rock and snow overlays: the detail that darkens cliffs, rock and snow in a pass of its own.

More dithering (16 colours and the 16- and 8-shade greys): the shades between the steps come back as a fine error-spread pattern (Floyd–Steinberg) instead of bands; the N64's filtering softens it. It is per texture and per map.

Every option is the real texels: made by libdragon's mksprite as the converter makes them, then decoded. NOW marks what the cartridge uses today.

Halo 64 texture picker: objects and terrain

What the options are

Objects (weapons, vehicles, characters, scenery): the same formats as the levels' picker — full colour (RGBA16), 16 colours (CI4: what they are now), greyscale (I4; IA4 for cut-outs) and greyscale tinted. A texture with the player's colour has its 16 colours split 8 + 8 now; the other options would lose the player's colour unless the renderer learns another way. First-person weapons and hands are drawn from an atlas cut into windows, so theirs have twice the texels.

Ground grain: the detail map drawn over the ground's colour tiles. It shares the texture memory with the 2 KB tile, so it can be 2 KB at most. Shown over the ground's average colour, as the renderer draws it (light × (0.5 + grain)). "+ micro detail" multiplies Halo's finer micro detail map into the grain (now only its average brightness is kept).

Rock and snow overlays: the detail drawn over cliffs, rock and snow in a pass of its own, darkening by its texel. Shown over the surface's average colour. The 4 KB options leave no room for mipmaps, so far away they shimmer.

Every option is the real texels: made by libdragon's mksprite as the converter makes them, then decoded. NOW marks what the cartridge uses today.

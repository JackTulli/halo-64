Halo 64 texture picker

What the options are
OriginalHalo's texture (up to 256 a side), for comparison
Full colour 32×64RGBA16, 15-bit colour; 4 KB, all of the RDP's texture memory
16 colours 64×64CI4, a 16-colour palette, the image's proportions; 2 KB + palette — what the colour textures are now
Greyscale 64×64I4, 16 greys (IA4, 8 greys + cut-out, for alpha-tested textures); 2 KB, leaves half the texture memory free
Greyscale 128×64the same at twice the texels; 4 KB — what the grey textures are now
… tinteda greyscale option times one colour fitted to the original; free on the N64 (the tint rides on the vertex colours)

Every option is the real texels: made by libdragon's mksprite as the converter makes them, then decoded. Shown at the original's brightness. NOW marks what the cartridge uses today.
